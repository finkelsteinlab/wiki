Glucose degrades rapidly in the autoclave and will cause variations in cell growth rates. It is best to prepare YES media as follows (see also [Microbial Media Recipes](/wiki/recipes/bacterial-media-recipes/#5-yes-media)):

## Contents

1. [YES Broth](#1-yes-broth)
2. [YES plates](#2-yes-plates)

## 1. YES Broth

Contents for 1L:

- 5 g YE (Yeast Extract)
- 30 g D(+) Glucose
- 225 mg Adenine
- 225 mg L-Leucine
- 225 mg Uracil

Add double distilled water to 1 L, mix well with stir bar and sterilize by filtering with a 0.2 µm vacuum filter cup.

## 2. YES plates

1. Autoclave 20 minutes at 110°C a 1L glass bottle containing 500 ml double distilled water, 20g of agar and a stir bar.
2. Cool down the autoclaved media at 60°C, add 500 ml of filtered 2x YES Broth and mix well with stir bar.
3. Pour 25 ml per Petri dish. Let dry O/N. Store at 4°C in the dark.
