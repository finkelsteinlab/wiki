Protocol taken from paper by Minoru Suga and Toyomasa Hatakeyama “A rapid and simple procedure for high-efficiency lithium acetate transformation of cryopreserved S.pombe cells” Yeast 2005; 22: 799-804. For the standard fresh-culture LiOAc transformation protocol (e.g., for pREP plasmids), see [S. pombe transformation by lithium acetate](/wiki/protocols/yeast/s-pombe-transformation-by-lithium-acetate/).

## 1. Reagents and media

- 30% glycerol in 0.1M LiAc pH4.9
- 50% PEG4000 ( Sigma-Aldrich- 95904)
- minimal medium with low carbon source. Paper uses SLD 0.67% YNB, 0.5% glucose, but we have found that synthetic complete medium with 3% glucose and 6.7% YNB works also.
- 1/2 YE -\> 0.25% yeast extract (Apex - 20-255), 1.5% glucose (Acros - 50-99-7)

## 2. Cryopreservation of competent cells

1. Grow cells in minimal medium with low carbon source overnight at 30°C with shaking (220 rpm) to 10<sup>7</sup> cells (an OD<sub>595</sub> of 0.5).
2. Place cells on ice for 15 minutes before harvesting.
3. Pellet cells at 1600 x g for 5 minutes and wash 3 times in 1ml ice cold sterilized water.
4. Resuspend cells in ice cold 30% glycerol in 0.1M LiAc pH 4.9 to 10<sup>9</sup> cells/mL (i.e. 100x smaller volume than the original culture).
5. Make 50µl aliquots and incubate for 30 minutes on ice to allow glycerol to permeate cells, then place cells directly into -80°C freezer.

## 3. Transformation with cryopreserved competent cells

1. Thaw cells at 30°C in a water bath for 2 minutes.
2. Mix with 5-10ng of DNA (for plasmid transformation) or 1-2µg (for integration) and 145µl of 50% PEG4000.
3. Vortex gently and incubate at 30°C for 1 hour.
4. Heat shock at 43°C for 15 minutes, allow to cool to room temperature for 10 minutes.
5. The paper states that the following three steps are optional and don't affect transformation efficiency:
   1. *Pellet cells at 1600 g for 3 minutes and remove supernatant by aspiration.*
   2. *Resuspend in 500µl 1/2 YE Medium (0.25% yeast extract, 1.5% glucose).*
   3. *Incubate at 30°C for 1 hour with shaking.*
6. Spread directly onto minimal selection plates and incubate at 30°C. If using drug selection, plate first onto YES agar plates and allow cells to grow into a lawn (2-5 days) before replica plating onto media with drugs.

## 4. Notes

For plasmid transformation expect several hundred colonies with this protocol. For chromosomal integration I have had varying results, from below 10 to nearly 200 colonies per plate.
