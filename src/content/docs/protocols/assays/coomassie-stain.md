## 1. Solutions

### 1.1 Coomassie Staining Solution (500 mL)

- 50 mL Glacial Acetic Acid
- 225 mL Methanol
- 225 mL H2O
- 1.25g Coomassie Brilliant Blue R250

### 1.2 Destain Solution (2L)

- 200 mL Glacial Acetic Acid
- 900 mL Methanol
- 900 mL H2O

## 2. Procedure

1. Stain gel for 5 - 30 minutes (overnight OK).
2. Wash gel 2x in water to remove excess dye.
3. Destain gel in Destain Solution for 1-2 hours.
4. Store gel in water once destained.
