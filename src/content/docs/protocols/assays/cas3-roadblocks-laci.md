## 1. Preparation

:::caution[Important Info]

- Need to make and purify PBamp lysogen \#386
- Use the [lambda DNA purification protocol](/wiki/protocols/dna-and-cloning/lambda-dna-purification/)
- Use LacI in BB10.4c (11 uM). Colored tubes are stocks. Clear tubes are 10 uL aliquots.

1. Make a working dilution of LacI (63 nM) by diluting 2 uL of stock into 350 uL BSA buffer - DTT.

:::

## 2. Imaging buffer

Use default roadblocks conditions.

- 40 mM Tris
- 0.2 mg/ml BSA
- 2 mM MgCl2
- 50 mM NaCl
- 1 mM ATP
- 1:1000 saturated biotin solution

## 3. Protocol

1. Add 10 nM Cascade final to 50 uL of site B cassette lambda DNA.
2. Incubate at 55°C for ten minutes.
3. Cool off to room-temp for five minutes.
4. Add 2 uL of LacI (63 nM) to the DNA and incubate on ice for 5 minutes.
5. Dilute to 1 ml injection and inject as normal.

## 4. Injections

- Injection 1: 1 uL anti-HA antibody (1:10 dilution) + 1 uL anti-rabbit QD 705 + 1 uL anti-FLAG antibody (1:10 dilution) + 1 uL strep QD 605.
  1. Incubate 10 minute and dilute into 150 uL Imaging buffer.
- Injection 2: 1.5 uL Cas3 into 150 uL Imaging buffer.
