## Day 0 prior to transfection
1. Lift HEK293T cells (10cm) with trypsin and pellet them at 250g for 5 min RT.
2. Discard supernatant and resuspend in 10 ml of DMEM.
3. Adjust cell density to 2.4×10^5  cells/ml with DMEM.
4. Seed 500uL of 2.4×10^5  cells/ml cells into 24-well plate.
 
## Day 1 Transfection
1. Prepare Mix-A and Mix-B. 3 ul of Lipo2000 for 1 ug of DNA.
Mix-A: each 50 ul of Opti-MEM (86)
Mix-B: 4300 ul of Opti-MEM with 258 ul of Lipo2000 
For each well 
Incubate 20 min at room temperature
100ul of mixture into each well
 
## Day 4 Cells harvest
1. Aspirate medium and wash cells with 200ul of PBS once for each well.
2. Resuspend in 500 ul of PBS and centrifuge @250g 5min. (can do it on the bench)
3. Discard supernatant and store cell pellet at -80 for genomic preparation.
4. Extract gDNA is followed Genomic extraction kit. 200 uL of ddH2O elution.

# PCR Gel extraction protocol:
1. Excise the DNA fragment from the agarose gel with a clean, sharp scalpel. 
2.	Weigh the gel slice in a colorless tube. Add 400 uL of QG buffer to your samples. (Add 3 volumes Buffer QG to 1 volume gel (100 mg gel ~100 μl). The maximum amount of gel per spin column is 400 mg.)
3.	Incubate at 50°C for 10 min until the gel slice has completely dissolved. Vortex the tube every 2–3 min to help dissolve gel.  
4.	Place a QIAquick spin column into a vacuum manifold. To bind DNA, apply the sample to the QIAquick column and apply vacuum to the manifold until all the samples have passed through the column. For sample volumes >800 μl, load and apply vacuum again. 
5.	To wash, add 750 μl Buffer PE to QIAquick column and apply vacuum. Centrifuge the QIAquick column in the provided 2 ml collection tube for 1-2 min to remove residual wash buffer. 
6.	Place QIAquick column into a clean 1.5 ml microcentrifuge tube. 
7.	To elute DNA, add 50 μl ddH2O to the center of the QIAquick membrane and centrifuge the column for 1 min. Let column stand for 1 min, and then centrifuge for 1 min. Increasing the incubation time to up to 4 min can increase the yield of purified DNA if you need.
