---
title: "Cell transfection process"
---

Day 0 prior to transfection
	Lift HEK293T cells (10cm) with trypsin and pellet them at 250g for 5 min RT.
	Discard supernatant and resuspend in 5 ml of DMEM.
	Adjust cell density to 2.4×10^5  cells/ml with DMEM.
	Seed 500uL of 2.4×10^5  cells/ml cells into 24-well plate.
 
Day 1 Transfection
3 ul of Lipo2000 for 1 ug of DNA.
The amount of reagent is for duplicated.
Mix-A: each 50 ul of Opti-MEM (86)
Mix-B: 4300 ul of Opti-MEM with 258 ul of Lipo2000 
 
*Single well 
Incubate 20 min @RT
100ul of mixture into each well
 
Day 4 Cells harvest
	Aspirate medium and wash cells with 200ul of PBS once for each well.
	Resuspend in 500 ul of PBS and centrifuge @250g 5min. (can do it on the bench)
	Discard supernatant and store cell pellet at -80 for genomic preparation.
 
Extract gDNA is followed Genomic extraction kit. 200 uL of ddH2O elution.
 
PCR reaction by KOD one
