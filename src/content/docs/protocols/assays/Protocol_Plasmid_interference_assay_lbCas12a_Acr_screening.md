**Bacterial plasmid interference assay for Acr screening**

This protocol is modified based on previous work (PMID: 34644300).

All plasmid protection assays were done in _E_. _coli_ (strain: BL21 DE3). LbCas12a was expressed via the arabinose-inducible promoter pBAD on a CloDF13-based plasmid marked with a spectinomycin resistance cassette. The LbCas12a construct was designed to eliminate an empty target vector, pZE21, with a KanR cassette, and the target vector expressing a gene of interest (e.g., an _acr_) via the doxycycline-inducible, pZE21-tetR. The expression was induced from the target vector via depression of the TetR transcription factor with doxycycline. IPTG was used in samples with the target vector to ensure high levels of TetR expression (which was driven by the lac promoter) and thus inducible control of the gene of interest.

1. Day 1.

First transformation by heat shock

_E. coli_ BL21 (DE3) strain was transformed with effect plasmids using heat shock.

the effector plasmid lbCas12a with target different spacers: pIF1107.

1. Day 2.

Pick up one colony from each plate and inoculate into 5 mL LB culture containing 50 μg/ml spectinomycin. Grow the cells at 37 C shaking at 220 rpm for overnight.

1. Day 3.

- Prepare cells for electroporation.

After overnight incubation at 37 C in 220 rpm shaker, 5 mL cells were transferred into 100 mL LB broth. Grow the cells at 37 C shaking at 220 rpm to an OD200 of approximately 0.5-0.7.

Chill cells on ice for 20 mins. For all subsequent steps, keep the cells as close to 0 C as possible and chill all containers in ice before adding cells. To harvest, transfer the cells to two cold Falcon tubes and spin at 4000 xg for 15 minutes a 4 C.

Carefully pour off and discard the supernatant. It is better to sacrifice the yield by pouring off a few cells than to leave any supernatant behind.

Gently resuspend the pellet in 50 mL of ice-cold 20% glycerol for each Falcon tube. Centrifuge at 4000 xg for 15 minutes at 4 C; carefully pour off and discard the supernatant.

Resuspend the pellet in 25 mL of ice-cold 20% glycerol. Centrifuge at 4000 xg for 15 minutes at 4 C; carefully pour off and discard the supernatant.

Resuspend the pellet in 20 mL of ice-cold 20% glycerol. Centrifuge at 4000 xg for 15 minutes at 4 C; carefully pour off and discard the supernatant.

Resuspend the pellet in a final volume of 1-2 mL of ice-cold 20% glycerol.

Freeze cells and stored at -80 C.

- Second transformation by Electroporation

For each sample to be electroporated, place a 1.5 mL microfuge tube and a 0.1 cm electroporation cuvette on ice.

In a cold, 1.5 ml microfuge tube, mix 40 ul of the cell suspension with 100 ng pTarget plasmid. Culture 1: pIF1080 (pZE21\_TetR\_GFP as negative control)

Culture 2: Acr-plasmid Library (eg. pZE21-tetR-gA21-28).

Mix well and incubate on ice for 5 minutes.

Set the micropulser to ‘Ec1’ when using the 0.1 cm cuvettes. Setting it to Ec2 when using 0.2 cm cuvette.

Transfer the mixture of cells and DNA to a cold electroporation cuvette and tap the suspension to the bottom. Place the cuvette in the chamber slide. Push the slide into the chamber until the cuvette is seated between the contacts in the base of the chamber. Pulse once.

Remove the cuvette from the chamber and immediately add SOC medium to total 1 ml to the cuvette. Quickly but gently resuspend the cells with a pipette.

Transfer the cell suspension to a 1.5 ml tube and incubate at 37 C for 1-3 hours, shaking at 220 rpm.

The cells from Culture 1 and 2 were added into 5 mL LB culture containing 50 μg/ml spectinomycin, 50 μg/ml kanamycin, and 0.5 mM IPTG. These growth conditions kept both LbCas12a and the gene of interest in uninduced states.

1. Day 5.

For the plates: The plates were photographed, and colonies were counted to determine the proportion of colony-forming units (CFUs) that remained kanamycin resistant. This proportion is a measure of how many colonies retained their target plasmid and, thus, how effectively that plasmid withstood LbCas12a attack. The log-transformed proportion of KanR/total CFU will be calculated for both the conditions with or without LbCas12a induction. Inhibitory activity of Acrs library is calculated by the ratio of between the proportion of KanR/total CFU in the E.coli with LbCas12a induction.

Make small cultures of the colonies for solid selection: Collect all colonies carefully from M1 and M2 on P2 plate for the Acr library. Prepare DNA samples for DNA sequencing following the above description in liquid selection.
