## 1. Before You Begin

Urea and carbamylation of proteins: With heat and time, urea can break down into ammonium cyanate. Isocyanic acid will attack primary amines in proteins (Arg, Lys, N-terminus) to make a carbamylated protein. This interferes with histone structure and function. Many early or sloppy in vitro studies have suffered from this problem for decades. Source: <http://www.ionsource.com/Card/carbam/carbam.htm>
