## 1. Summary

The tape that makes the best DNA curtains is the 3M 665 tape that has the 400 and 1070 adhesives. Acrylic adhesives seem to be fine for making good DNA curtains. This pattern was taken with a polyester tape called Double Tack Mounting FILM [y_chennel_presentation.pptx](/wiki/media/careers/y_chennel_presentation.pptx)

## 3. Results

- <img src="/wiki/media/careers/adventures-with-tape.png" width="200" alt="adventures-with-tape.png" />
- 3M 666 behaves the same as 3M 656 which is the regular uPVC tape we use all the time.
- 3M 9576 cleaves dsDNA and makes shorter curtains.
