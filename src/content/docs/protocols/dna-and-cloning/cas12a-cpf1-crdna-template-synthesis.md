This is basically just PCR with a few minor changes. You should work under RNAse-free conditions since the product will be put directly into the T7 RNA transcription reaction overnight.

## 1. Reaction setup

50 µL reaction volumes:

- Water: 33.5 µL H<sub>2</sub>O
- 5X Q5 Reaction Buffer: 10.0 µL
- 10 mM dNTPs: 1.0 µL
- 10 µM Forward Primer: 2.5 µL
- 10 µM Reverse Primer: 2.5 µL
- Q5 Polymerase: 0.5 µL

## 2. PCR program

- 98°C 0:05
- 10 cycles of:
  - 98°C 0:05
  - 57°C 0:30
  - 72°C 1:00
- 72°C 2:00
- Hold at 4°C

## 3. Gel check and cleanup

**IMPORTANT**: Have one separate control lane without polymerase. The differences in size between extended and non-extended DNA is small. Run a 3% TBE agarose gel with the control and all products, using NEB Low Molecular Weight ladder.

Do PCR cleanup with the MinElute Purification Kit. It’s like a regular PCR cleanup but optimized for short templates. Columns are in the door of the 4°C chemical fridge.
