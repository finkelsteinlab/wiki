## 1. End-labeling reaction

### 1.1 Reaction mix

Make a 40 ul reaction:

- 29 ul diH2O
- 4 ul 10x T4 PNK Buffer (*contains MgCL2)
- 4 ul NEB Lambda DNA HindIII Digest Ladder [500ng/ul]
- 2 ul T4 PNK Enzyme
- 1 ul Gamma 32P-ATP
- 40 ul Total Volume

### 1.2 Incubation and cleanup

1. Incubate reaction 1 hour at 37°C.
2. Heat inactivate T4 enzyme at 65°C for 20 minutes. Cool.
3. Apply reaction (50 ul max volume) to Monarch PCR Clean Up Kit for larger than 50 bp to remove unincorporated Gamma 32P-ATPs.
   1. Discard radioactive column in radioactive dry waste.
4. Store radioactive end-labeled DNA Lambda HindIII ladder in radioactive -20°C freezer.

## 2. Alkaline ladder

- 6ul Lambda HindIII end-labeled ladder
- 9 ul diH2O
- 3 ul 6X Alkaline Loading Dye
- 18 ul Total Volume

Load 2-6 ul on Alkaline Agarose gel.

## 3. Alkaline gel loading dye (6X)

- 45 ul 10M NaOH
- 18 ul 0.5M EDTA
- 0.33g Ficoll 400
- 0.003g Bromocresol Green Dye
- 0.004g Xylene Cyanol Dye
- QS to 1.5 mls with diH2O
