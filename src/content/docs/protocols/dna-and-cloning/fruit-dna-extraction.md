This is a good lab for teaching lessons on DNA. It also uses a variety of techniques to keep things interesting. Plus the lab smells like fruit after.

## Contents

1. [Materials](#1-materials)
2. [Protocol](#2-protocol)

## 1. Materials

- 50g of fruit
- plastic baggies
- Extraction buffer: 250 mM NaCl and 10% SDS
- Isopropyl alcohol

## 2. Protocol

1. In a plastic sandwich bag, add 30g of fruit and 25ml of extraction buffer.
2. Mix the solution by smashing the fruit in the bag for 3 minutes.
3. Let the mixture rock for 10min.
4. Separate the solids from the liquids by centrifugation at 9,000 Gs for 10 minutes.
5. Add 0.7 v/v of cold IPA to precipitate the DNA.
6. Use a bent glass rod to spool out the DNA.
