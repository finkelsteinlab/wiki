## 1. Competency test procedure

Greg Hild measured their competency by transforming the plasmid pUC19 into the cells at 5 ng, 500 pg, and 50 pg using the following procedure:

1. Defrost the cells from -80C on ice (approximately 20 minutes).
2. Add 50 uL of cells to an Eppendorf tube and 1 uL of the appropriate plasmid and flick the tube 6 times to mix.
3. Ice for 5 minutes, heat shock in a 42C water bath for 42 seconds and then ice for 2 minutes.
4. Add the 50 uL of cells to 500 uL of SOC and shake in the INNOVA shaker at 37C, 250 rpm for 1 hour.
5. Plate 300 uL of cells and allow to grow in the incubator at 37 C for 24 hours.
