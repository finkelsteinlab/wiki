## 1. Storage guidelines

:::caution

ATPgS and AMP-PNP are **very** expensive. Take special care when handling these analogs. Also note that ATPgS is contaminated with ~15% ADP (as indicated on Roche ATPgS insert)

:::

(d)NTPs will degrade if not stored properly. Degraded nucleotides may screw up your PCR/replication assays. If you follow these simple guidelines, your dNTPs will be stable for years:

- Aliquot in small, 10-20uL tubes
- Avoid multiple freeze-thaw cycles
- Store at a concentration above 10 mM
- **store at -20C**
- Always keep on ice and do not store at 4C for any length of time
