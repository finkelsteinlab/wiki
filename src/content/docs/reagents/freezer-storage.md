## 1. Plasmid Database

Store plasmids in a 1.7 mL centrifuge tube by assigning them the next available **pIF#** in the Google Drive database, printing a label, and storing them in the appropriate `-20°C` box. Include the concentration of the plasmid on the side of the tube for ease of access.

When adding a plasmid to the database, record:

1. **pIF number:** Next sequential number (e.g., if the last plasmid entered was `pIF20`, yours is `pIF21`).
2. **Description:**
   - Name of the gene
   - Original vector backbone
   - Primers used to construct the plasmid
   - Orientation of the gene of interest
3. **Strain number:** Corresponding `IF#` glycerol stock (see below).
4. **Initials and date.**
5. **Resistance markers.**
6. **Sequence map** uploaded to the database folder.

## 2. Strain Database

To freeze *E. coli* strains for long-term `-80°C` storage:

1. In a 2 mL cryotube, mix either:
   - **1 mL** saturated overnight culture with **1 mL** sterile 50% glycerol (25% final), or
   - **5 volumes** of overnight/exponentially growing culture with **1 volume** sterile 80% glycerol (~13% final).
2. Flash-freeze in liquid nitrogen and store in the appropriate `-80°C` rack. Strains stored this way remain viable for >25 years.

When adding a strain to the Google Drive database, record:

1. **IF number:** Next sequential `IF#`.
2. **Strain background** (e.g., DH5α, DH10Bac, Rosetta(DE3)pLysS, ArcticExpress).
3. **Plasmid carried** (`pIF#` and gene of interest).
4. **Resistance marker.**
5. **Initials and date.**

## 3. Oligo Database

Oligos are stored at **100 µM** upon receipt in the IDT tube they arrived in. Resuspend the dried oligo in ddH2O (or TE), assign it the next number in the Google Drive database, print a label, and store it in the appropriate box in the `-20°C` freezer.

## 4. Racks Database (-80°C Freezer)

When a protein is purified or received externally, or when you add new cell pellets or boxes of any kind, aliquot and store them in a labeled box in the `-80°C` freezer:

- Each `-80°C` freezer is designated by a double letter (`AA`, `BB`, `CC`, etc.).
- Each rack is numbered (`1`, `2`, etc.).
- Each rack has 5 rows (`1–5`) and 4 box depth positions (`a–d`). For example, the third row of the second rack in the front-most box position is labeled **`2.3a`**.

When adding or removing a box, update the Racks Database in Google Drive with:

1. **Freezer and rack location** (e.g., `AA 2.3a`)
2. **Date made**
3. **Description of contents**
4. **Your initials** (when a box is emptied, remove the physical box and clear its slot in the database)
