## 1. Overview

<img src="/wiki/media/instruments/fisherwaterbath.jpg" width="250" alt="Water Bath" />

We have two water baths in the lab, one in the main room and one in room 3.422BA. They each have two compartments set to different temperatures.

:::caution

Always use DI water in the baths. That prevents funk from growing in the water.

:::
