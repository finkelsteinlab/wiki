## 1. Overview

<img src="/wiki/media/instruments/drill_press.gif" width="350" alt="Servo Drill Press" /> We purchased a drill press that is used in the jewelry industry. We use the drill press for drilling points into microscope slides to establish a flow cell. The drill bits we used are 1.4 mm diamond coated drill burrs. These cost \$4.80. The quartz slides you will use cost \$15.00 ea. Please don’t use the drill bit to the last of its life. You will break the slide.

- [Shor Diamond Coated Twist Drill Burs](https://www.ishor.com/DrillsDiamond.php), 1.4mm (catalog: DIB-211.00)

## 2. Maintenance

- Make sure the drill press and base are completely dry after drilling – **prevent rust**.
- The manual is a hardcopy. It will always be next to the drill press.
