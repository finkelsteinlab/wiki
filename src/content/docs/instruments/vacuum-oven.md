## 1. Overview

<img src="/wiki/media/instruments/vac_oven.jpg" width="150" alt="Vacuum Oven" /> The Finkelstein lab uses the vacuum oven to keep debris from adhering to our precious slides. There will always be a vacuum linked to the vacuum oven so please make sure the door is properly sealed using vacuum grease. The vacuum oven will always be on. Always.

## 2. Operation

1. Turn on the vacuum pump.
2. Make sure the knobs for vacuum and purge are turned clockwise all the way.
3. Turn on the vacuum oven and set temperature to 150°C, about 10 on the temperature knob.
4. Slowly open the vacuum knob.
5. When slides need to be added or removed, close the vacuum knob and open the purge the chamber via the purging knob.
