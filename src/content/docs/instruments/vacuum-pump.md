## 1. Overview

<img src="/wiki/media/instruments/rotaryvane.jpg" width="250" alt="Rotary Pump" /> The vacuum pump in the lab was setup by Nacho. It is connected to the lyophilizer, vacuum oven, and vacuum desiccator through a metal tee. Always leave the vacuum on and change the oil when necessary.

## 2. Operating procedure

1. Ensure all valves are in neutral position or closed (including pump T-valve, vacuum desiccator T-valve, and vacuum oven valves).
2. Turn on pump.
3. Direct the vacuum by turning the pump T-valve to the **PUMP** direction.
4. Open the vacuum valve of the vacuum oven or vacuum desiccator.
5. Wait at the pump until the built-in vacuum pump gauge reads solid green.
6. Close vacuum valve.
7. Bleed the pump by turning the pump T-valve towards the **BLEED** direction.
8. Turn off the pump.
9. Ensure all valves are in neutral position or closed.
