## 1. Overview

These instruments comprise a comprehensive and flexible gel electrophoresis system that effectively separates nucleic acids using submerged agarose gels. We have 2 Wide Mini-Sub Cell GT systems and 2 Mini-Sub Cell GT systems.

**Keep the gel systems clean!**
