## 1. Overview

<img src="/wiki/media/instruments/analytical_bal.jpg" width="150" alt="AnalyticalBalance" /> <img src="/wiki/media/instruments/preciso_bal.jpg" width="150" alt="PrecisionBalance" />

We have two analytical balances and two precision balances in the laboratory. The analytical balance has a draft shield and is more accurate when measuring small quantities. These are FACT (Fully Automatic Calibration Technology) balances. This means we never have to calibrate them! If you don’t trust this or assume magic is at hand, I recommend reading 4.8.1 in the manual.
