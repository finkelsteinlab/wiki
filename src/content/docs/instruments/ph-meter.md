## 1. Overview

<img src="/wiki/media/instruments/fep20.jpg" width="250" alt="fep20.jpg" /> The pH meter in the lab is the Five Easy Plus. pH meters need general maintenance every week. Please keep the electrode clear of salty build up and make sure the tip of the electrode is in pH (KOH) solution. We have several buffers to calibrate the meter. Use the instruction manual as a guide for calibration.
