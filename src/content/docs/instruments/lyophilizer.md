## 1. Overview

<img src="/wiki/media/instruments/freezone_lyophilizer.jpg" width="450" alt="Lyophilizer" /> The lyophilizer is used in conjunction with the Labconco Vacuum pump to freeze dry things (berries) using low pressure and temperature. Please see manuals for maintenance and use. It is a joint purchase between the Finkelstein lab and the Miller Lab. **Please take good care of it**.
