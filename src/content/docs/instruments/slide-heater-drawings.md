## 3. Getting parts made

- [Mechanical Engineering machine shop](https://me.utexas.edu/?p=1220), ETC 1.210C, me-machine-shop@utexas.edu. Its page lists a flat rate of $51/hr and charges funded research groups for consultations. Ask before sending work, since the page doesn't say whether it takes jobs from other colleges.
- Chemistry [Instrument Design & Repair](https://chemistry.utexas.edu/research/facilities), WEL 2.130, handles electronic and electromechanical work, not machining.
- Drawings for the black throne and the Prior H230 slide stage are on vendor templates; ask the lab manager for copies.
