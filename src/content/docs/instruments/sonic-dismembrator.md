## 1. Sonic Dismembrator

<img src="/wiki/media/manuals/sonicator.jpg" width="150" alt="Sonic Dismembrator" />

The sonicator is used to disrupt membranes using high frequency vibrations.

We have a Fisher Scientific Model 705 Sonic Dismembrator. This is a re-branded [Qsonica Sonicator Q700](http://www.sonicator.com/sonicatorQ700.aspx) <a class="pdf-copy" href="/wiki/media/archive/www-sonicator-com-sonicatorq700-aspx.pdf" title="Archived PDF copy">PDF</a>. Fisher did not have their manual online, so the Qsonica manual is attached.

### 1.1 Available tips

- 1/4” tip – for lysis of up to 250 ml of cell culture
- 1/16” microtip – for lipid sonication only.

### 1.2 Manual

- [Sonic Dismembrator Manual](/wiki/media/manuals/sonicator_manual.pdf)

### 1.3 Maintenance

- Make sure that you wipe all ice spills and leave the tip clean.

## 2. Bath Sonicator

<img src="/wiki/media/instruments/branson_2510.jpg" width="150" alt="Bath Sonicator" />

The bath sonicator is useful for cleaning various instruments and using ultrasonic frequencies to break apart cells.

**Always have water in the bath.**
