## 1. Overview

<img src="/wiki/media/instruments/nanodrop2000c.jpg" width="150" alt="NanoDrop 2000c" /> The NanoDrop 2000c is used for routine and low-volume UV-Vis applications. Its great for measuring the concentration of DNA or protein in your sample. We also use it for [Bradford Assays](/wiki/protocols/assays/bradford-assay/).
