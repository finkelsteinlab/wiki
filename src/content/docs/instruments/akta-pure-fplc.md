## 1. Overview

<img src="/wiki/media/instruments/akta_pure_photo.jpg" width="200" alt="akta_pure_photo.jpg" />The AKTA system is great for purifying proteins. We have many different columns that should suit your needs. Affinity columns, Q and SP, Gel Filtration columns for size exclusion and a set of metal columns (just cobalt (pink)). Feel free to pack your own columns and make great proteins/do good science.

**Serial Number:** 2038115

For operating procedures, column catalog numbers, and before/after checklists, see [AKTA Purifier](/wiki/instruments/akta-purifier/), [FPLC 101: BSA Practice Run](/wiki/instruments/fplc-101-bsa-practice-run/), and [FPLC Checklist](/wiki/instruments/fplc-checklist/).
