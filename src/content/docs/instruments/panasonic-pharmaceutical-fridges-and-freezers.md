## 1. Overview

<img src="/wiki/media/instruments/panasonic_pharmaceutical_fridge_mpr-1411.jpg" width="200" alt="panasonic_pharmaceutical_fridge_mpr-1411.jpg" /> The Panasonic MPR-1411-PE is a Large Capacity Laboratory Refrigerator. Stable and reliable for exacting laboratory requirements. Ideal temperature environment for clinical research, pharmaceutical and industrial use. Adjustable shelves and wide range set point for varying laboratory applications including chromatography and pharmaceutical storage. With a temperature range of 2°C to 23°C, the Panasonic MPR-1411-PE refrigerator is ideally suited for many tests that require a stable, cool temperature or general cold storage
