Independent funding is the corner-stone of a successful scientific career. Whether you’re an undergrad, graduate student or postdoc, you need to be writing grants.

## 1. Grant writing resources

On July 12, 2013 UT Austin hosted the Grant Central seminar, “Write Winning Grant Proposals”. The Grant Central folks have an excellent series of booklets for writing grants. Although the focus is on PI-level proposals, there are tons of useful info in the booklets:

- [NIH Grants Workbook](/wiki/media/manuals/nih_grant_writing_workbook.pdf)
- [NSF Grants Workbook](/wiki/media/manuals/nsf_grant_writing_workbook.pdf)
