Communicating your work clearly is one of the most important aspects of being a good scientist. The references below will help you to improve your writing skills.

## 1. Resources on Scientific Writing

- [The Art of Writing Science](/wiki/media/careers/art_writing_science.pdf) (Kevin Plaxco) - a wonderfully short and punchy introduction on writing manuscripts for publication.
- [How to Write Your First Research Paper](/wiki/media/careers/write_first_paper.pdf) (Elena D. Kallestinova) - a good breakdown for beginners
- [Whitesides' Group: Writing a Paper](https://doi.org/10.1002/adma.200400767) (George M. Whitesides) - very short outline of how to structure a research paper
