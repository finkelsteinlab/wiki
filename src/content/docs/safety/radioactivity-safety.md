## 1. Overview

Finkelstein Lab is authorized to use 32P. Radioactivity is restricted to specific areas in the lab. The designated areas are described and mapped in the lab's Radioactive Material Application, kept in the radiation binder.

The radioactive material binder is kept in the lab and contains current radioactive materials inventory and disposal information, daily user survey logs, and weekly contamination survey logs.

## 2. Link to UT Radiation Safety Website

Here you can find the UT Radiation License as well as Forms, Policies and Guidelines related to radioactivity in the lab and a link to the State’s regulations.

<http://www.utexas.edu/safety/ehs/radiation/>

## 3. Safety

All lab employees must complete UT mandated Radioactive Safety Training (OH 301 Basic Radiological Health) upon acceptance into the lab and periodic refresher courses to comply with UT regulations. <http://www.utexas.edu/safety/ehs/train/courses.html>

Proper safety equipment must be worn/utilized while working with radioactive materials.

[Radioactive License User Commitments](/wiki/media/protocols/radioactive_materials_license_commitments.pdf)
