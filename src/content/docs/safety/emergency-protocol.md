## 1. In case of emergency

In case an emergency (fire, bomb threats etc.) follow Universities instructions.

## 2. In case of evacuation

1. Leave your experiments and leave the lab in an orderly fashion.
2. **Make sure all bunsen-burners and other fire-causing lab equipment is turned off.**
3. Send an e-mail or text message to Ilya immediately so he can make sure you’ve evacuated safely.

Please know the contact information for the different lab members.
