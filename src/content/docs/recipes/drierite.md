## 1. Usage

Drierite is blue when dry and purple when exposed to humidity.

## 2. Regeneration

1. Spread used granules about 1 inch deep in shallow pans.
2. Heat in an oven at 230° C for 2 hours to achieve complete dehydration.
3. The regenerated material should be placed in the original container and sealed while hot.
4. Drierite will become less effective after repeated regenerations.

## 3. Disposal

1. Place granules in a sealed container that will prevent dust from being created, then throw away in the regular trash.
