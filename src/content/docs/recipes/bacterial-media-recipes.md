## 1. Super Optimal Broth (SOB)

| Component | Amount | Final concentration |
|:---|:---|:---|
| NaCl | 0.5 g | |
| tryptone | 20 g | |
| deionized H2O | to 950 ml | |
| yeast extract | 5 g | |

1. Shake until the solutes have dissolved.
2. Add 10 ml of a 250 mM solution of KCl. (This solution is made by dissolving 1.86 g of KCl in 100 ml of deionized H2O.)
3. The pH of the medium should be near pH 7.0. Check and adjust with 5 N NaOH.
4. Adjust the volume of the solution to 1 liter with deionized H2O.
5. Sterilize by autoclaving for 20 minutes on liquid cycle.
6. Just before use, add 5 ml of a sterile solution of 2 M MgCl2. (This solution is made by dissolving 19 g of MgCl2 in 90 ml of deionized H2O. Adjust the volume of the solution to 100 ml with deionized H2O and sterilize by autoclaving for 20 minutes at 15 psi \[1.05 kg/cm2\] on liquid cycle.)

Recipe from [CSH Protocols](http://cshprotocols.cshlp.org/content/2006/1/pdb.rec8085.full?sid=e5313002-0dfe-4d6f-9135-cda260df45f4)

## 3. Terrific Broth (TB)

Used mainly for growing high densities of DH5a or similar cells to produce high concentrations of plasmid DNA. Recipe taken from [CSH protocols](http://cshprotocols.cshlp.org/content/2006/1/pdb.rec8620)

Make the following separately:

### 3.1 Salts Solution

Final concentration: 0.17 M KH2PO4, 0.72 M K2HPO4

1. This solution is made by dissolving 23.1 g of KH2PO4 and 125.4 g of K2HPO4 in 900 mL of H2O.
2. After the salts have dissolved, adjust the volume of the solution to 1000 mL with H2O and filter the solution.

### 3.2 TB solution

| Component | Amount | Final concentration |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Deionized H2O | to 900 mL | |
| Tryptone | 12 g | |
| Yeast extract | 24 g | |
| Glycerol | 4 mL | |

1. Shake until the solutes have dissolved.
2. Sterilize by autoclaving for 20 min on liquid cycle.
3. Allow the solution to cool to 60°C or less, and then add 100 mL of a sterile salts solution from above.

| Bacto Yeast Extract | 10g | |

1. Adjust pH to 7.0 with 10N NaOH.
2. Adjust to 1L using distilled H20.
3. Sterilize by autoclaving.

## 5. YES Media

| Component | Amount | Final concentration |
|:---|:---|:---|
| ddH2O | to 1L | |
| YES media | 36.13 g | |

1. Stir for 10 minutes.
2. Use a 0.2 uM membrane filter (disposable filter) and attach it to the bottle near an open flame.
3. Vacuum filter sterilization into a 1L bottle.
4. Detach the filter next to the flame and flame the cap before placing it onto the bottle.

## 6. SC Media

| Component | Amount | Final concentration |
|:---|:---|:---|
| ddH2O | to 1L | |
| drop out mix synthetic minus uracil without yeast nitrogen | 1.92 g | |
| yeast nitrogen base without amino acid | 6.7 g | |
| glucose | 20 g | 2% |

1. Heat with stirring until dissolved.
2. Adjust to desired pH if needed.
3. Sterilize with 0.2 uM membrane filter (disposable filter).

Note: unless making SC-leu, be sure to add 225 mg/L leucine to these when using -leu dropout mix if the SC powder does not contain it already
