## 1. TAE

**T**ris, **A**cetate, **E**DTA is used routinely for agarose electrophoresis of nucleic acids. The 1X working solution is 40 mM Tris-acetate/1 mM EDTA pH 8. We will make 5L of a 10x TAE concentrate and dilute to 1x prior to running a gel.

**10x** TAE: For 1 L

1. Dissolve 48.4 g Tris base in around 600 mL of ddH2O.
2. Slowly add 11.42 mL glacial acetic acid.
3. Add 20 mL 0.5 M EDTA, pH 8.
4. Bring up the volume to 1 L with ddH2O.

## 2. TBE

**T**ris, **B**orate, **E**DTA is used routinely for polyacrylamide electrophoresis of nucleic acids. The 1X working solution is 89 mM Tris-borate / 2 mM EDTA, pH 8.3 (0.5X is 44.5 mM Tris-borate / 1 mM EDTA). We usually make a 5X TBE stock (or 5 L of 1X TBE). Higher concentrations of TBE (5X and 10X) tend to precipitate out of solution over time.

Recipe (1L)(pH 8.3):

| Component          | 5X TBE  | 10X TBE |
|:-------------------|:--------|---------|
| Tris Base          | 54.0 g  | 108 g   |
| Boric Acid         | 27.5 g  | 55 g    |
| 0.5M EDTA (pH 8.0) | 20.0 mL | 40 mL   |

## 3. TGS

**T**ris, **G**lycine, **S**DS is used for SDS-PAGE denaturing protein gels. The 1X working solution is 25 mM Tris-chloride, 192 mM glycine and 0.1% SDS, pH ~8.6. We will make 5L of a 10x TGS solution and dilute it to 1x prior to running protein gels.

Recipe (2L):

| Component  | Powder to add | Final Concentration |
|:-----------|:--------------|:--------------------|
| Tris Base  | 60.4 g        | 250 mM              |
| Glycine    | 288.0 g       | 1.92 M              |
| SDS Powder | 20.0 g        | 1%                  |
