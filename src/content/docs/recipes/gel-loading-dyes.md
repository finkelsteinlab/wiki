The gel loading dye helps to increase the density of your samples so they stay in the well. A tracer dye is useful to visualize filled wells and gives an estimate of progression of electrophoresis. Finally, for SDS-PAGE gels, the SDS present in the loading dye helps to denature proteins.

## 1. Agarose Loading Buffer (6x)

Common dyes and how they approximate linear DNA on an agarose gel. These are very rough estimates.

| Dye | 0.5-1.5% agarose | 2.0-3.0% agarose | CAS number | Sigma Catalog \# |
|:---|----|:---|----|----|
| Xylene cyanol | 10-4 kbp | 750-200 bp | 2650-17-1 | Sigma X4126 |
| Cresol Red | 2-1 kbp | 200-125 bp | 62625-29-0 | Sigma 114480 |
| Bromophenol blue | 500-400 bp | 150-50 bp |  | Sigma B8026 |
| Orange G | \<100 bp | ? |  | Sigma O3756 |

The precise amount of dye is not important. However, it is advisable that you prevent overlay of dye and expected DNA size. For example, if you are expecting a band of 200-400nt, bromophenol blue will obscure your product a bit.

### 1.1 Recipe

**Sucrose & xylene cyanol / bromophenol blue (6x)** Combine:

| Component | Amount | Final concentration |
|:---|:---|:---|
| sucrose | 4g | |
| bromophenol blue | ~20mg | |
| xylene cyanol | ~20mg | 0.25% |
| dH2O | to 10mL | |

**Storage:** Store at 4°C to avoid mould growing in the sucrose. 10mL of loading buffer will last for years.

## 2. SDS-PAGE Loading Buffer (4x)

For preparation and loading of protein samples onto a gel for SDS-PAGE analysis (Western blot/protein blot). SDS contained in the sample buffer makes proteins negatively charged proportionally to their length. 2-mercapto-ethanol/DTT breaks disulphide bonds. Glycerol makes the sample buffer more dense than the surrounding running buffer of the protein gel, enabling easy loading into the gel pockets.

### 2.1 Recipe

To make 10 mL of 4x stock, combine:

| Component | Amount | Final concentration (1x) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 1M Tris-HCl pH 6.8 | 2.0 ml | 50 mM Tris-HCl pH 6.8 |
| SDS | 0.8 g | 2% SDS |
| 100% glycerol | 4.0 ml | 10% glycerol |
| 14.7 M β-mercaptoethanol | 0.4 ml | 1% β-mercaptoethanol |
| 0.5 M EDTA | 1.0 ml | 12.5 mM EDTA |
| bromophenol Blue | 8 mg | 0.02 % bromophenol blue |

**Storage:** Store in aliquots at -20oC.

Use of loading buffer: Dilute protein sample 1:3 into 4X sample loading buffer. Load on acrylamide gel in SDS-PAGE buffer.

:::caution

β-mercaptoethanol is a severe irritant, is readily absorbed through the skin and smells terrible. Please never open the stock bottle outside of a hood.

:::
