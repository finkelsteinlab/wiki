## 1. Overview

For routine cloning and plasmid preparations, we use the traditional [alkaline-lysis method](/wiki/protocols/dna-and-cloning/plasmid-mini-prep-alkaline-lysis/). When large quantities of high-quality plasmids need to be made, we use the [Promega Wizard Midiprep Kit](http://www.promega.com/products/dna-and-rna-purification/plasmid-purification/wizard-plus-midipreps-dna-purification-system/) for high-purity plasmid preparation. The columns and resin are purchased from Promega, while the buffers are made in-house.

Midi-prep manuals:

## 2. Recipes

When making the buffers, make sure to write your initials and the date on the appropriate buffer bottle. If a buffer is running low, make more for the lab!

### 2.1 Cell Resuspension Buffer

**To make 500 mL of buffer:**

| Component           | Volume to add | Final Concentration |
|:--------------------|:--------------|:--------------------|
| Tris-Cl pH 7.5 (1M) | 25 ml         | 50 mM               |
| EDTA pH 8 (0.5M)    | 10 ml         | 10 mM               |
| RNAse A (solid)     | 50 mg         | 100 µg/ml           |
| H2O                 | to 500 ml     |                     |

### 2.2 Cell Lysis Buffer

**To make 500 mL of buffer:** NaOH Molecular Weight: 40 g/mol

| Component    | Volume to add | Final Concentration |
|:-------------|:--------------|:--------------------|
| NaOH (solid) | 4 g           | 0.2 M               |
| SDS (solid)  | 5 g           | 1 %                 |
| H2O          | to 500 ml     |                     |

### 2.3 Neutralization Buffer

**To make 500 mL of buffer:** Potassium Acetate Molecular Weight: 98.15 g/mol. Adding glacial acetic acid will solidify your potassium acetate mixture. Add water to place it back in solution.

| Component                 | Volume to add | Final Concentration |
|:--------------------------|:--------------|:--------------------|
| Potassium Acetate (solid) | 64.8 g        | 1.32 M              |

1. Dissolve 64.8 g Potassium Acetate in ~30 ml H2O.
2. Add glacial acetic acid to bring to pH 4.8 (~ 100 mL acetic acid).
3. Bring up to 500 mL with H2O.

### 2.4 Column Wash Buffer

**To make 4 L of buffer:** Dissolve everything in water and add ethanol at the end. If using 95% ethanol, adjust water concentration accordingly to get a final ethanol concentration of 55% v/v.

| Component                 | Volume to add | Final Concentration |
|:--------------------------|:--------------|:--------------------|
| Potassium Acetate (solid) | 31.4 g        | 80 mM               |
| Tris-Cl pH 7.5 (1 M)      | 33 ml         | 8.3 mM              |
| EDTA (0.5 M)              | 0.32 ml       | 40 µM               |
| H2O                       | 1.76 L        |                     |
| Ethanol (100%)            | 2.2 L         | 55%                 |
