| Compound | Concentration | Volume | Recipe/Notes |
|:---|----|:---|----|
| BSA | 20 mg/mL | 50 mL | Found in the 4 degree fridge. Add 1.0 g BSA to 50 mL water and vortex to mix. Syringe filter into a new conical tube and aliquot 1 mL into 1.7 mL tubes. |
| IPTG | 1 M | 50 mL | Found in the -20 degree fridge. Add 11.915 g IPTG to 50 mL water and vortex to mix. Syringe filter and aliquot 1 mL. |
| DTT | 1 M | 50 mL | Found in the -20 degree fridge. Add 7.71 g DTT to 50 mL water and vortex. Syringe filter and aliquot 1 mL. |

All of the aliquots should be placed in their appropriate box into the -20 degree fridge. Use the label maker to label the aliquot tubes with the name, concentration and date it was made.
