## 1. -Leu Sc Media Plates

1. 250 mL of 4% agar solution (10g) and autoclave for 20 minutes on liquid cycle.
2. 250 mL of 2X -Leu SC media: 0.81 g -leu dropout mix, 3.35 g YNB, 10 g glucose.
3. Heat with stirring until very warm.
4. Filter sterilize.
5. Mix -leu SC media with 4% agar solution.
6. Wait until cool enough and pour onto plates.
