| Buffer | Volume | Recipe/Notes | Vendor | Catalog Number |
|:---|----|----|----|----|
| 3M NaCl | 1L | Dissolve 175.32 g NaCl in ~800 mL ddH2O. Bring up to 1 L after stirring and vacuum filter. | VWR | BDH9286-12kg |
| 5M NaCl | 1L | Dissolve 292.2 g NaCl in ~800 mL ddH2O. Heating can help dissolve the salt faster. Bring up to 1L after stirring and vacuum filter | See above |  |
| 0.1 M NaOH | 1L | Dissolve 4 g NaOH in ~800 mL ddH2O. Bring up to 1L after stirring and vacuum filter. | EMD Millipore | SX0590-3 |
| 0.1 M HCl | 1L | Add 8.33 mL of 12M HCl in 991.667 mL ddH2O. Add Acid to water and vacuum filter. | Fischer Scientific | A144-500 |
| ddH2O | 1L | vacuum filter |  |  |

The 3M NaCl is for the back microscope room, and the 5M NaCl is for the front microscope room. The re-usable vacuum filter should be used and cleaned after use. The 2L microscope buffer bottles should be switched out with a new one after 3 or 4 buffer refills.
