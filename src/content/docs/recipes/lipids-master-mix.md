## 1. Reagents

Purchase reagents as powders in individually sealed ampules.

- DOPC – [850375P](http://avantilipids.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=231&Itemid=207&catnumber=850375)
- DOPE-biotin – [870273P](http://avantilipids.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=778&Itemid=183&catnumber=870273)
- DOPE-mPEG2K – [880130P](http://avantilipids.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=1089&Itemid=448&catnumber=880130) <a class="pdf-copy" href="/wiki/media/archive/avantilipids-com-index-php.pdf" title="Archived PDF copy">PDF</a>
- Chloroform

## 2. Procedure

1. Make the following stocks from the products above by dissolving the powder in chloroform:
   - 100 mg/ml DOPC
   - 25 mg/ml DOPE-biotin
   - 100 mg/ml DOPE-mPEG2K
2. Combine the above stocks as follows:
   - 1 ml DOPC (97.7 mol%)
   - 20ul DOPE-biotin (0.3 mol%)
   - 80ul DOPE-mPEG2K (2 mol%)

## 3. Notes

- Master mix prepared as above will make enough for five batches of lipids. Use 220ul of master mix per batch of lipids.
- Make sure to use Hamilton syringe with teflon gasket to avoid dissolving rubber or plastics into the chloroform.
- All lipids should be stored at -20C
