## 1. Protocol Lab Book

Molecular Cloning book is awesome and routinely referenced in many labs. Most protocols have a great introduction explaining the fundamentals.

## 2. Protocols Online

- [CSH Protocols](http://cshprotocols.cshlp.org) is the online version of the Lab manual. There are significantly more protocols available, but its not as convenient to browse as a book. Here is a sample of protocols we will use routinely:
  - [Optimizing and troubleshooting PCR](http://cshprotocols.cshlp.org/content/2009/4/pdb.ip66.full)
  - [Quantitation of DNA and RNA](http://cshprotocols.cshlp.org/content/2007/11/pdb.ip47.full)
  - [Agarose gel DNA electrophoresis](http://cshprotocols.cshlp.org/content/2006/1/pdb.prot4020.full)
  - [Plasmid miniprep by Alkaline Lysis](http://cshprotocols.cshlp.org/content/2006/1/pdb.prot4084.full)
- [Current Protocols in Molecular Biology](http://www.currentprotocols.com/WileyCDA/CurPro3Title/isbn-0471142727,descCd-tableOfContents.html) is another excellent source for general biochemistry protocols. The chapters below are a very useful general reference:
  - [Introduction to E. coli, plasmids, etc](http://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/0471142727.mb0100s59/full)
  - Take a look at Chapters 1, 2, 3 to get started
- [OpenWetWare](http://openwetware.org/wiki/Main_Page) is a Wikipedia-style collection of protocols from different labs from across the world. This site is great for looking at variations in a given protocol from lab to lab.
