### 2.1 First login

How to I log on for the first time? If you have not used the mailing list system before, go to the main UT Lists page and select the First Login link. Enter your e-mail address and click the Send Me My Password button. Your new password will be sent to your e-mail address, and you can log on using it.

### 2.2 Lost password

Help! I’ve lost my password!

To obtain a new password, select the Lost Password link on the main UT Lists page, then enter your e-mail address and click the Send Me My Password button. A new password will be sent to that e-mail address immediately.

### 2.3 Subscribing

Login Use the search bar to do a search and subscribe to the lists you are interested in subscribing to. After finding the list; subscribe after you are subscribed you will receive e-mail for this group list.

### 2.4 Unsubscribing

To Unsubscribe: Login Select the subscription from the left hand menu. “Under Subscriber Options” on the left hand menu click “Unsubscribe”
