## 1. Put in Quartzy

- Biostore purchases (give receipt to lab manager)
- NEB Now freezer (forward email receipt to lab manager and add request to quartzy)
- Procard purchases (give quote to lab manager to order or attach receipt in Quartzy. IMPORTANT: mark them as received in Quartzy when they arrive)
- Purchases from any vendor except IDT

## 2. Do NOT put in Quartzy

- Anything from IDT
