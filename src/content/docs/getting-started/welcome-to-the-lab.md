### 1.2 Do Online

1. Ask the lab manager to add you to the [finkelsteinlab](https://github.com/finkelsteinlab) GitHub organisation so you can edit this wiki.
2. **Complete Lab Safety Classes**
   - <http://ehs.utexas.edu/training/lab-training-requirements.php>
   - UT EID is required
   - ALL lab members take courses OH101, OH102 (taught by supervisor), OH201, OH202, OH 204, OH207, OH 241 and FF205 (1 hour fire extinguisher course)
   - Complete OH304: Laser Safety
   - Complete OH301: Basic Radiological Health
   - Complete OH218: Bloodborne Pathogens (only if you need to use the Tissue Culture room)
   - <u>Safety courses will be retaken every 3 years</u>
   - Click on <http://ehs.utexas.edu/training/lab-training-requirements.php> to see your training history and to find new classes.

## 3. Working in the Lab

1. Introduce yourself to the [rancillo_silvia](/wiki/lab-life/rancillo-silvia/).
2. Familiarize yourself with proper use of lab instruments.
3. Read about how to maintain a [lab_notebook](/wiki/getting-started/lab-notebook/).
4. Learn the system for storing plasmids, oligos, strains, and proteins. [freezer_storage](/wiki/reagents/freezer-storage/)
5. Some protocols for [getting_started_at_the_bench](/wiki/getting-started/getting-started-at-the-bench/)
6. **Do Good Science!**
