## 1. Preparation

1. Plug in waffle maker.
2. Spray surfaces with oil.
3. Combine mix with water in a 4:3 ratio (2:1 if you prefer a thicker waffle)
4. Wait for the "ready" light to illuminate.
5. Pour mix into the center.
6. Remove waffle when it stops steaming. Using the wooden mixing stick seems to be the optimal tool.
7. Wash the plastic cup and wooden stick.
8. Enjoy your waffle.
