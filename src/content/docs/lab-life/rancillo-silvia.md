## 1. General Information

- The Rancillo Silvia V3 is the top of the line model for semi-automatic machines.
- Espresso is produced by forcing a small amount of nearly boiling (97C) water through ground coffee at 9 bars of pressure.

## 2. Protocol for Espresso

1. Grind coffee in Baratza Virtuoso Preciso at espresso level.
   - 1-14 Macro-adjust settings and an extra dozen micro-adjust settings.
   - Experiments are ongoing to determine ideal grind settings – need more info.
   - So far the best grind setting is between 8g and 9f.
2. Grind coffee into the portafilter.
3. Remove excess and tamp the grinds down.
4. Lock portafilter onto espresso machine. Make sure there is water in the machine.
5. Flip the switch and watch espresso pour out.
   - Let espresso run for about 20-25 secs.
   - Should be a blonde in color at the end.
6. **Clean portafilter and drip pan!**
7. Additional information: [YouTube instructions](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QhxvDusY3jk)

:::caution

Clean the filter and drip tray after every shot

:::

## 3. Protocol for a Latte

So you are tired of the classic and bold flavor of an espresso and you want something with a more complex lighter flavor, perhaps for the morning. Enter the latte.

1. Make your shot of espresso using the espresso protocols and using a latte cup.
2. For the latte, switch on the steamer button and purge the steam wand by rotating the knob counter-clockwise.
   - Let the water drip out until only steam is produce. Turn the knob back.
   - It’s a lil scary.
3. Fill the milk pitcher halfway with soy milk and place the tip of the steam want in the milk.
4. Froth the milk until the temperature is around 38C.
5. Slowly, so as not to disturb the crema of your perfect shot, pour the milk into the latte.
6. End with some zazzle.
