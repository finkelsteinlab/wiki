# Finkelstein Lab Wiki

Protocols, instruments, recipes and lab know-how from the Finkelstein Lab at UT Austin, published at <https://finkelsteinlab.org/wiki/>.

- To add or change a page, read [Editing the wiki](src/content/docs/contributing/editing-the-wiki.md).
- Pages are Markdown in `src/content/docs/`; pictures and PDFs are in `public/media/`.
- Every push to `main` rebuilds the site (GitHub Actions, about two minutes).
- To preview locally: `npm ci`, then `npm run dev` and open <http://localhost:4321/wiki/>.

Migrated from the lab's DokuWiki in October 2026. Older revisions are kept in the lab's offline backup; each page's footer lists who edited it and when.
